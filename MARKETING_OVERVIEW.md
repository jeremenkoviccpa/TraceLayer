## What is TraceLayer?

TraceLayer is a **continuous compliance evidence collection SaaS** for startups and scale-ups pursuing security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS, NIST CSF).

It connects to the tools a company already uses — AWS, GitHub, Okta, Jira, Slack, Google Workspace, and more — and automatically pulls compliance evidence 24/7, so teams are always audit-ready instead of scrambling for weeks before an audit.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Getting certified (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.) is a requirement for closing enterprise deals. But the evidence collection process is brutal:

- Engineering teams spend **weeks manually exporting screenshots, access logs, and change records**
- Evidence goes stale between audits — you're compliant one day, not the next
- Tools like Vanta cost **$15,000–$30,000/year**, pricing out early-stage startups
- Audit prep is a distraction from building the product

**Result**: startups either lose enterprise deals because they can't show compliance, or burn months on manual audit prep.

---

## Who We Sell To (ICP)

**Primary buyer**: Startup CTO or Head of Engineering at a B2B SaaS company

- **Company stage**: Series A or pre-Series A, 10–100 employees
- **Trigger**: A prospect or enterprise customer has asked for a SOC 2 report
- **Pain**: They know they need it, they don't have time to do it manually, and they can't afford the big players
- **Goal**: Get certified as fast as possible with minimal engineering distraction

**Secondary buyer**: Compliance officer or IT manager at a mid-market company that has outgrown their current compliance tooling.

## Suggested Email Campaign Structure

**Sequence: 5 emails over 3 weeks**

1. **Email 1 (Day 1)** — Problem-aware opener. Hit the enterprise deal blocker angle. One ask: "Is SOC 2 on your roadmap this year?"
2. **Email 2 (Day 4)** — Differentiation. Compare the old way (Vanta costs + manual prep) vs TraceLayer.
3. **Email 3 (Day 8)** — Social proof / outcome. What does audit-ready actually look like? Reference the AI audit package output.
4. **Email 4 (Day 14)** — Objection handling. "We're too early for this." / "We'll do it manually." / "We're already using Vanta."
5. **Email 5 (Day 21)** — Soft close / breakup email. Last chance, low-pressure ask.

## Tone & Voice

- **Professional but not stuffy** — we're talking to technical founders, not lawyers
- **Specific over vague** — "pulls 847 access log entries from Okta" beats "collects evidence"
- **Respect their time** — short emails, one clear CTA per email
- **Never use** "cutting-edge", "game-changing", "revolutionary", "synergy"
