# TraceLayer — ICP & List Building Brief

> Give this document to your list builder. They need to deliver:
> **First name, Last name, Job title, Company, Work email, LinkedIn URL**
> Verified emails only. No info@, no hello@.

---

## Who We're Targeting

### The Company

| Attribute | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Type** | B2B SaaS company |
| **Size** | 10–200 employees |
| **Stage** | Pre-seed → Series B (ideally Seed or Series A) |
| **Revenue** | $0–$10M ARR |
| **Geography** | United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia |
| **Industry** | Software / Technology / SaaS |
| **Sells to** | Enterprises or mid-market companies (their customers are businesses, not consumers) |

**Why this company?** They are selling to enterprise customers who will eventually require a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate before signing a contract. They need compliance but can't afford Vanta ($20k/year).

---

### The Person (Decision Maker)

Target **one of these titles** per company — in this priority order:

1. **CTO** (Chief Technology Officer) ← highest priority
2. **Co-founder & CTO**
3. **VP of Engineering**
4. **Head of Engineering**
5. **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** ← if the company has one
6. **Head of Security / Director of Security**

**Do NOT target:** CEO (unless they are also technical co-founder), HR, Marketing, Sales, Finance.

---

## Buying Signals — Prioritize Companies With Any of These

These are signs the company is about to need compliance. If the list builder can filter for these, prioritize them:

- Recently raised a funding round (Seed or Series A in the last 12 months)
- Job postings for: "Security Engineer", "DevOps", "Compliance Manager", "CISO"
- Their website mentions: "SOC 2", "ISO 27001", "enterprise", "security", "trust"
- They sell into healthcare, fintech, government, or legal sectors
- They have a /security or /trust page on their website

**Where to find funding signals:** Crunchbase, TechCrunch, LinkedIn

---

## Where to Find These People

Tell your list builder to use these sources:

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — filter by: Title, Company headcount, Industry, Geography
- **Apollo.io** — same filters, can export with verified emails
- **Crunchbase** — filter companies by funding stage and date, then find contacts
- **Hunter.io** — to verify or find emails once you have company domains

---

## Negative ICP — Do NOT Include

Exclude these even if they match other criteria:

- Companies with 200+ employees (they already have compliance tools)
- Non-SaaS companies (agencies, consulting firms, hardware, physical products)
- B2C companies (their customers are consumers, not businesses)
- Companies that already publicly advertise SOC 2 certification (they're already solved)
- Government agencies or non-profits
- Free tools / open source projects with no revenue
- India, Eastern Europe, Southeast Asia (outside our current geo focus)

## Volume & Quality Expectations

- **Minimum:** 500 contacts
- **Target:** 1,000–2,000 contacts
- **Email validity:** 90%+ deliverability (ask them to verify with Hunter or NeverBounce before delivery)
- **No duplicates**
- **One contact per company** (the most senior technical person)

---

## What to Tell the Fiverr Seller

Copy-paste this brief or say:

> "I need a B2B lead list of CTOs and VPs of Engineering at B2B SaaS startups with 10–200 employees, Seed to Series B stage, based in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia. Verified work emails required. I'll send you this brief with the full criteria. I need 1,000 contacts delivered in a spreadsheet."

**Budget expectation:** $50–$150 for 1,000 verified leads depending on the seller. Don't pay more than $0.15 per verified contact. Ask for a sample of 10–20 before paying for the full list.

---

## How to Vet the Fiverr Seller

Before ordering:
- [ ] Check their reviews — look for "B2B", "SaaS", "LinkedIn" in past review text
- [ ] Ask: "What tool do you use to verify emails?" (Good answer: Hunter, NeverBounce, ZeroBounce)
- [ ] Ask for a free sample of 10 leads matching the ICP above
- [ ] Don't pay for the full order until you've verified the sample quality
