## Tab 1 — Compliance Consultants & vCISOs

**Target:** Individual freelancers and small firm owners (1–10 people) who manage SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or GDPR programs on behalf of multiple client companies. This is the highest-priority list.

**Target count:** 50 leads

### Who qualifies

- Freelance compliance consultants or vCISOs with their own client base
- Owners/founders of boutique compliance advisory firms (1–10 employees)
- People who explicitly mention managing compliance "for clients" or "for multiple companies" — not in-house compliance people managing their own company
- Must be actively working (recent LinkedIn activity, recent posts, or updated profile in last 6 months)

### Who does NOT qualify

- In-house compliance officers, CISOs, or security managers at a single company
- Big 4 / Big consulting firm employees (Deloitte, PwC, KPMG, EY, Accenture) — wrong profile
- Compliance software salespeople
- Auditors (those go in Tab 2)

### LinkedIn search — Sales Navigator filters

Run these searches and combine results (deduplicate):

**Search 1 — vCISO**
- Title keywords: `vCISO` OR `virtual CISO` OR `fractional CISO`
- Seniority: Director, VP, Owner, Partner, CXO
- Company headcount: 1–10
- Geography: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia (prioritize US)

**Search 2 — Compliance Consultant**
- Title keywords: `compliance consultant` OR `SOC 2 consultant` OR `GRC consultant` OR `ISO 27001 consultant`
- Seniority: Director, VP, Owner, Partner, CXO, Self-employed
- Company headcount: 1–10

**Search 3 — Fractional / Advisory**
- Title keywords: `compliance advisor` OR `security advisor` OR `CISO advisor` OR `fractional security`
- Seniority: Owner, Partner, Self-employed
- Company headcount: 1–50

### Additional sources

- **Clutch.co** → search "cybersecurity consulting" → filter by 1–10 employees → grab firm owners
- **LinkedIn company search** → "vCISO" as company name keyword → grab owners/founders
- **ISACA member directory** (if accessible) → CISM/CRISC certified members with "consultant" in title
- **Upwork** → search "SOC 2 consultant" → top-rated freelancers → find their LinkedIn

### Disqualify if

- Title is "Chief Information Security Officer" at a company with 50+ employees (in-house, not consultant)
- Works at a Big 4 or large consulting firm
- Profile is clearly a job seeker (open to work banner)

## Priority Order

1. **Tab 1 — Consultants** (50 leads) — highest ROI, most likely to pay $99/client immediately
2. **Tab 2 — Auditors** (50 leads) — free listing ask, warm lead pipeline benefit
3. **Tab 3 — Pen Testers** (50 leads) — free listing ask, referral deal conversation

## Notes for Researcher

- Do NOT include anyone at a company with 1,000+ employees unless they are explicitly a freelancer/consultant
- Verify each firm actually does SOC 2 or compliance-related work — check their website services page
- For email: use Apollo.io or Hunter.io. If no email found, LinkedIn URL is sufficient
- Flag any prospect who has publicly posted about compliance frustrations, vCISO work, or SOC 2 on LinkedIn — note it in the Notes column
- Aim for quality over quantity — 50 verified contacts beats 200 with bounced emails
